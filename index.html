My Dearest,

I am deeply sorry for all the arguments and misunderstandings we've had over these past few weeks. Knowing that I caused you pain or upset you weighs heavily on me every single day.

I take full responsibility for my actions and words. Being far away from you is already tough, but knowing that you are hurt because of me makes the distance feel unbearable. You mean everything to me, and hurting you is the last thing I ever wanted to do.

Please take all the time you need. I will be right here, waiting patiently to talk to you whenever you feel ready.

Forever Yours, Ridesh ❤️
